// Features

What you can do
right now.

Hyzeer is still early, but the core stuff is here: track rounds, manage discs, add courses, and help fill in the database while we keep fixing and improving things.

Game Creation & Scoring
Start a solo round, play with friends, or open a public match. Keep score hole by hole and look back at past rounds anytime.
Disc Bag Management
Keep your bag in one place with flight numbers, colors, stability notes, and a few reminders about what each disc is good for.
Course Database
The course list is still growing. If your local layout is missing, add it so the next player can find it.
Open Data
Courses, discs, and public rounds feed the shared API. If you build tools around disc golf data, you can use it too.
Open Public Games
Start a public match on a course and let nearby players join. Setup is simple and scoring stays easy to follow.
Lost & Found
Put a QR code on your disc so someone can reach you if they find it. They do not need the app.
Real‑time Player Counts Coming Soon
See how busy a course is before you head out.
Social Community Feed Coming Soon
Share rounds, comment on them, and keep up with what friends are playing.
Challenges & Achievements Coming Soon
Set goals, track progress, and give yourself a few things to chase.

// Lost Disc Network

Lost disc?
We've got you.

Put a QR sticker on your disc so the finder can reach you without installing anything.

1

Register Your Disc

Add your disc to your digital bag, generate its QR code, print it, and stick it on — quick setup, no lengthy forms.

2

Apply Weatherproof QR Sticker

Stick the QR code on your disc. You can print your own for now, and later we plan to offer tougher stickers made for actual outdoor use.

3

Get Notified Instantly

When someone scans the code, they can contact you right away. They do not need the app.
