// Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

No paywalls. No subscriptions. Your data is never sold. Hyzeer is free, and shared data can power community tools through the Hyzeer API. We just launched, so add your courses, discs, and rounds to help everyone grow.

Game Creation & Scoring
Start solo rounds, invite friends, or jump into public matches. Real-time scoring, hole-by-hole tracking, and round history always available.
Disc Bag Management
Track every disc in your bag with flight numbers, colors, stability ratings, and notes. Filter by driver type and know exactly what you're throwing.
Courses data
The database starts empty — help us build the world’s largest open‑data course catalogue. Add your local layouts and watch the network grow, free for everyone to use.
Open Data
Every course and disc you log is instantly added to our open API. Rounds too — if you have a public profile. Build powerful integrations and tools on top of community data.
Open Public Games
Open a match on any course and let nearby players jump in — no signup, no organizer, just pure action and fair play.
Lost & Found
QR stickers connect discs back to you. Finders scan, you get notified — no app required on their end.
Real‑time Player Counts Coming Soon
See how many folks are currently on course and make split‑second decisions about where to play.
Social Community Feed Coming Soon
Post round results to your friend group, like and comment on rounds, and shape a living community feed built by players.
Challenges & Achievements Coming Soon
Track goals, earn badges, and push your game through structured challenges.

// Lost Disc Network

Lost disc?
We've got you.

QR stickers link your disc back to you — no app required for the finder. The more players register, the stronger the network gets.

1

Register Your Disc

Add your disc to your digital bag, generate its QR code, print it, and stick it on — quick setup, no lengthy forms.

2

Apply Weatherproof QR Sticker

Stick the durable QR code on your disc — you can print your own sticker now, or at a later date buy rugged, weather‑proof stickers from us. It survives crashes, mud, water, and forest encounters.

3

Get Notified Instantly

When a finder scans the QR code, you're connected immediately — no app required for the finder.

Register. Stick. Relax.
One QR sticker connects your disc to you — forever
